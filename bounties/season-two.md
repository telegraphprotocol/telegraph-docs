# Bounties and Season II

Season II is one source of public work on the bounty board. It is not the whole board and does not limit the kinds of contributions Telegraph can need.

Season II-related Issues use `source:h2`. They may also identify an app mission and the reusable Intents or capabilities that would unblock it. The Issue’s acceptance criteria still define the work; a mission label is context, not proof that a capability is live.

Browse [Season II issues](https://github.com/telegraphprotocol/bounties/issues?q=is%3Aissue+source%3Ah2), or use the public bounty repository’s mission map for its current catalogue.
