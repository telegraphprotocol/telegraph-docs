# Rules and Rewards

## The rules

1. **Work only on the Intents in the bounty's table**, using their exact names.
2. **Use only Intents that already exist.** See [below](#only-intents-that-already-exist).
3. **Be honest.** Use real questions, check every fact, and only add a source
   link the material really came from. Don't let an AI make up material for you.
4. **Share only what you're allowed to share.** No personal data, private
   documents, passwords or secrets, in your material or in comments.
5. **Don't try to see or guess the hidden test material**, and don't try to
   trick the checks, for example with near copies of the same question.
6. **Be kind.** Comments follow the board's
   [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/telegraphprotocol/bounties/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Only Intents that already exist

Every bounty uses Intents that are already in the Telegraph catalogue or on the
network. Nobody proposes or adds a new Intent through a bounty, and no bounty
will ask you to.

If an Intent you know well has no bounty, you can still add material for it on
Anjoman. The network's evaluator rewards (below) don't depend on a bounty.

## Staying safe

> [!WARNING]
> Nobody from Telegraph will ever ask for your **password, private key,
> recovery phrase or money**. Anyone who does is trying to scam you. Don't reply,
> and report it.

- Report security problems, leaked secrets and scams **privately**: email
  **info@telegraphprotocol.com** with the subject
  `[SECURITY] Telegraph vulnerability report`. Never post them in a public
  comment.
- Everything on the bounty board is public. Write comments as if anyone could
  read them.

## Rewards

### The bounties themselves

Right now every bounty is **unfunded**, and carries the label `reward:unfunded`.
No money or tokens are promised for it. It's still real work that the network
needs.

If a bounty is ever funded, its **Reward** section will state the amount, the
asset paid and the rules before anyone can claim it. When you claim, the bot
copies the Reward section into its reply, so the terms you joined under are on
record even if the bounty is edited later. The full rules are in the board's
[REWARDS.md](https://github.com/telegraphprotocol/bounties/blob/main/docs/REWARDS.md).

### The network's evaluator rewards

Separately from any bounty, the network itself rewards evaluators every day in
MACHINA, its token. For each Intent, the reward is shared between the adapter
that ranks it and the people whose material it used. So when rankings use your
material, you earn a share. The split is on [Tokenomics](../protocol/tokenomics.md).

Today the network is a test network, so these rewards are paid in test tokens.

### Season II prizes

Hackathon prizes, tracks and dates are set by the official
[Season II rules](https://hackathon.telegraphprotocol.com/rules), not by the
bounty board.
